    Helpful Information

    Welcome to our Help Center. Take a look at some of the most common questions we’ve received from our customers. ​Come here when you need important treatment information, diet plans, workout videos, and other helpful resources. If you still have something you’d like to know, let us know and we’ll get back to you shortly.

    PRE-TREATMENT BODY SCULPTING INSTRUCTIONS

    • Avoid eating heavy meals on the day of your treatment. DO NOT eat for two hours before your treatment time.

    • DRINK WATER! Drinking plenty of water is critical to aid in the flushing out of broken-down fat excreted through your lymphatic system. Drink at least two liters of water the day before your treatment!

    • DO NOT consume ANY caffeine, alcohol, or carbonated drinks the day before treatment.

    • DO NOT fast, or your body will go into "starvation mode" and become more resistant to releasing stored fat. Instead, speak with your practitioner about a healthy diet and appropriate supplements such as Vitamin B Complex to help your body detox.

    • DO NOT wear lotion or any products on the area we are treating.

    • Shave any body hair on the area we are treating.

    • Wear comfortable clothing for your treatment, like leggings.

    THINGS TO KEEP IN MIND POST-TREATMENT

    • Wearing a waist trainer or any form of shape-wear after each session can maximize results!

    • Swelling, itching, and minor pain are normal post-treatment. Call your doctor if these symptoms last long-term or get significant.

    • You may see results as early as two weeks but do not be discouraged if you don't. Full results can take 1-3 months to finalize. The body will continue to excrete fat cells up to six months post-treatment.

    CANNOT HAVE ULTRASOUND CAVITATION

    • Pregnant

    • Breastfeeding

    • HIV/AIDS

    • Cancer (all forms)

    • Undergoing chemotherapy or immune therapy

    • Immunodeficiency

    • Lupus

    • High blood pressure (uncontrolled)

    • Diabetes (uncontrolled)

    • Prednisone and other steroid medications (treatment will increase inflammation)

    • Heart problems or diseases

    • Kidney damage, diseases or problems

    • Liver damage, diseases or problems

    • Haemorrhagic disease, trauma or bleeding

    • Numb or insensitive to heat

    • Active implanted device such as pacemaker or defibrillator

    NEED A DOCTOR'S APPROVAL LETTER

    • Epilepsy

    • Transplant Anti-Rejection Drugs

    • Thyroid Disease

    • Heart Disease

    • Hypertension (High blood pressure treated with medication)

    • Cancer - after 12 months

    WAITING PERIOD BEFORE TREATMENT

    • Recent surgery (wait 6 months)

    • Recent scars (wait 6 months)

    • Open lesions (avoid area)

    • Skin diseases (avoid area)

    • Infection (avoid area)

    • Antibiotics (wait until course completed)

    • Inflammation (avoid area)

    • Varicose veins (avoid area)

    • Prosthesis/silicone implants (avoid area)

    • Metal implants/screws (avoid area)

    • Menstrual Period (wait until after)

    • Metal contraceptive coil (avoid area)

    • Depilatory creams (wait 2 weeks)

    • Areas being treated with IPL or Laser (wait 2 weeks)

    • Areas being treated with Botox® (wait 3 months) or dermal fillers (wait 2 weeks)

    • Suntan (sun must be avoided the entire duration of the treatment regime)

    • Chronic inflammation (depending on the severity of chronic inflammatory conditions - consider that treatment will increase inflammation and anti-inflammatory medications reduce the effectiveness of the treatment - please discuss with your doctor first.
