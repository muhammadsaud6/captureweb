    WHY DO YOU NEED RED LIGHT THERAPY?

    Safe

    We are the only pad-based red light device that is FDA-cleared* for circumferential reduction.

    *Contour Light® is FDA-Cleared for both Body Contouring (circumference reduction), temporary pain relief from arthritis and muscle/joint pain, and locally increased blood circulation.

    Painless

    Contour Light Red Light® is painless. The only sensation you will feel is the warmth of the led lights.

    Fast & Effective

    Each session lasts up to 30 minutes, and you can see results from the first session!​

    It is backed by an IRB-approved clinical study where patients experienced an average loss of 2.49″ per session. 

    Painless and drug-free fat reduction

    Our Red Light technology:

    • Reduces fat in hard-to-reach areas like the thighs and back.

    • Is a long-term non-surgical fat reduction system.

    • Relieves muscle and joint pain.

    • Improves overall appearance.

    • Gives instant and long-term results.

    Discover a new you

    Contour Light®‘s revolutionary treatment helps you shape up your trouble spots by targeting fat deposits with light and heat. The light penetrates deep into the skin, stimulating the fat cells, and allowing them to release their stored triglycerides.
